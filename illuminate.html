Illuminate

Paste any untrusted string — a suspicious .md, an AI response, a string-slice pulled from a binary. Scrub the slider to inspect it through four lenses. Surfaces hidden zero-width characters, bidi overrides (Trojan Source), control chars, and homoglyph attacks. Everything runs in your browser — nothing leaves the page.

Why a hunter cares: attackers smuggle instructions into content an LLM will read — a zero-width run, a right-to-left override that reorders what a human sees vs. what the machine parses, a Cyrillic а wearing a Latin a's face. RAW shows what you think you have; ILLUMINATE strips it to a skeleton; DEEP SCAN names every hidden byte; RAINBOW makes anomalies pop. Defensive only — surface it, disclose it, never weaponize it.
