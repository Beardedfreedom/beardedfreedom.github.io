02B · GATOR GREENS

Eighteen holes in the land that's left

A full-size course needs 120 to 180 acres, which is the whole estate. After the twelve attractions, the setbacks and the buffers around buildings and water, about 59 acres are free. That is not enough for a regulation or an executive course, but it is room for an 18-hole par-3 course: par 54, 2,860 yards on 21.5 acres, with the Castle as the clubhouse and two loops of nine. The front nine runs through the south-east woods; the back nine weaves around the house compound.

Five holes make you carry water and five make you carry sand, every green has two or three bunkers, and the whole course is graded into the laser-measured ground as a 3D CAD model: greens raised and tilted, tees flat, bunkers and ponds dug, with the pond and bunker spoil building the greens and tees.

Fit test, not a final routing. The CAD file is masterplan/golf/golf_course_3d_EPSG2236_ftUS.dxf, and the course is also in the walkabout game. Parcel lines, irrigation water and tree removal need confirming, and a golf architect would refine the hole lengths, green sites and safety netting. The north-east quarter, where the plan put the RV camp, looks like neighbours' lots and is left out here.

04 · FOUR LANDS AROUND THE LAKE

Lakeside cabins

07 · THE GROUND

What the laser saw

The estate sits west of Robbins Road. The main house, 7,423 sq ft with a 31 ft ridge, is at the south end of the road with the cleared square, a ring and a pond behind it. Two lakes with a loop track lie 1,300 ft north. These images are shaded from the lidar surface; every roof, tree and bank is real, and the colours are land cover, not photographs.

08 · HOW IT WAS MADE

Public data, no Google Earth

Google Earth's 3D mesh can be downloaded in an afternoon, but Google's terms forbid exporting or deriving models from it, and it carries no accuracy specification. The public route is legal and more accurate.

  1. Locate. The address was matched to county address points and road geometry after a search-engine coordinate proved 1.7 km off.
  2. Lidar. 39.5 million points clipped from the USGS 3DEP index for Lake County (2018, public domain), reprojected to NAD83 Florida East, US survey feet, NAVD88.
  3. Base. Bare-earth and surface grids, 1 ft and 0.5 ft contours, footprints with lidar heights, tree crowns, lake outlines at water level, all as layered DXF and Civil 3D point files.
  4. Zones and homes. Equal-area sectors solved around the lake, pads placed under stated rules, parametric cabins exported as DXF blocks on the pads.
  5. Master plan. The poster's elements mapped to the parts of the estate that already fit them, as a plan image, DXF and JSON.
Concept grade. No parcel lines yet, zoning for district A unverified, the 50 ft water buffer is an assumption, and a boundary-plus-topo survey must replace all of this before any permit.
09 · NEXT

What it takes to make it real

Land and law

Parcel polygons for 09-21-26-000300004800, -4600 and -4900 from the Lake County Property Appraiser. A planned development or special-use approval for an amusement and camping use in district A, with noise, traffic and stormwater studies. Florida amusement-ride permits for anything mechanical, health department approval for the camp, and FAA review for a 40 ft tower and drone shows.

Design

A boundary and topographic survey. The first cabin taken to a real floor plan with a window schedule. A second cabin row with a rear lane. Stage, bathhouse and gate designs. Parking for 4,000 people, which this plan does not yet show and which will need 8 to 10 acres of field.
