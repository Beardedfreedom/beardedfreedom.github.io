Sausage Castle: from an address to a buildable idea

A 120-acre estate modelled from free government lidar, split into four equal lake zones, and filled with three kinds of themed tiny home. Everything on this page came from public data and a few Python scripts. No Google Earth model was touched.

01

The site as the laser saw it

The estate sits west of Robbins Road. The main house, 7,423 sq ft with a 31 ft ridge, is at the south end of the road, with a cleared compound of pads, a ring and a pond behind it. Two lakes with a loop track lie 1,300 ft north. The images below are shaded from the lidar surface, so every roof, tree and bank is real; the colours are land cover, not photographs.

03

Four equal zones around the lake

The zone lines radiate from the lake centre so each zone has exactly 2.04 acres between a 50 ft no-build water buffer and a limit 250 ft from the shore. The existing 1,640 ft loop track becomes the lane. Pads are 35 × 50 ft, 20 ft off the lane edge and 15 ft apart. Widening the ring to 300 or 350 ft adds no pads: the lane is the limit, and a second row would need a rear lane.

How it was made, and why not from Google Earth

Google Earth's 3D mesh can be downloaded in an afternoon, but Google's Maps Platform terms forbid exporting, storing or deriving 3D models from it, and the mesh carries no accuracy specification. The public route is legal and more accurate.

  1. Locate. The address was matched to county address points and road geometry after a search-engine coordinate proved 1.7 km off.
  2. Lidar. 39.5 million points were clipped from the USGS 3DEP Entwine index for Lake County (2018, public domain) and reprojected to NAD83 Florida East, US survey feet, NAVD88.
  3. Base. Bare-earth and surface grids, 1 ft and 0.5 ft contours, building footprints with lidar heights, tree crowns, lake outlines at their water level, all as layered DXF and Civil 3D point files.
  4. Zones. Equal-area sectors solved numerically around the lake, pads placed under stated spacing rules, capacity tested at three ring depths.
  5. Homes. Parametric massing models per theme, exported as DXF blocks and dropped on the pads at ground elevation, with concept images generated from the same specs.
Concept grade. No parcel lines yet (Lake County GIS was unreachable), zoning for district A unverified, the 50 ft water buffer is an assumption, and a boundary-plus-topo survey must replace all of this before any permit.

What happens next

Data

Pull the parcel polygons for 09-21-26-000300004800, -4600 and -4900 from the Lake County Property Appraiser, and check the district A zoning text for tiny homes, park models and waterbody setbacks.

Design

Pick the first unit to take to a real floor plan with wall thicknesses and a window schedule, decide on a second row with a rear lane, and add a shared bathhouse, parking and stormwater set-asides.
