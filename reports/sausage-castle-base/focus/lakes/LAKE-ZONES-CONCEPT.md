Drafted 2026-09-28 on the lidar base. Concept grade: no parcel lines, no survey, zoning not yet verified with Lake County.

## What to check before this becomes a plan

1. Lake County zoning for district A and whether tiny homes, park models or an RV-style community are permitted here, plus any Planned Unit Development route.
2. The county's waterbody and wetland setbacks (this concept assumes 50 ft) and St. Johns River Water Management District rules for the lake.
3. Parcel lines from the Property Appraiser, since the 250 ft ring may cross the estate's lots.
4. Septic or sewer, well capacity and fire access: a 20 ft loop with pads on both sides needs turnouts or a wider lane for fire apparatus.
